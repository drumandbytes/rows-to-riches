        The basics
              
        Press INSERT to add rows by hand. Fast presses build a combo, up to +100%. Spend rows on Data Sources, which produce rows on their own, even while the game is closed (up to your offline cap). Each card shows its share of your output. Need an exact number for an objective? Switch the buy bar to sell to sell sources back for half their price (free starter units can't be sold, and a sold source leaves the auto-buyer).
        
              
        Upgrades double sources, link neighbouring sources (synergies) or boost INSERT; the ones you own are listed under Deployed this run. Schema columns multiply everything, at a steep price.
        
              
        Data gravity: past 1 quadrillion rows/s, data gets heavy and extra production has diminishing returns (a square-root-ish curve). Bigger multipliers still help; they just help less. Stats shows how much gravity is costing you.
        
            
        Corporate life
              
        🎯 OKRs arrive every quarter (15 minutes of play) once you've re-platformed: three objectives scaled to where you are. Each one you hit gives a production bonus.
        
              
        📣 All-hands closes every quarter: your OKRs get graded, you get a performance rating (bonus included, sometimes), and leadership makes announcements.
        
              
        📅 Meetings and 📨 requests start after a few re-platforms: stakeholders want things, calendars fill up. Each card has two choices with different trade-offs and a 30-second timer. Ignoring it is also a choice.
        
            
        Incidents
              
        Every minute or two an alert pops up somewhere on screen. Click it before it disappears: production frenzies, instant backfills, INSERT storms and, much later, abductions.
        
              
        Two are different: phishing tests (free GPU credits, urgent password resets) should be ignored, and when the intern drops a table you have to click quickly to restore it, or lose 10% of your bank.
        
            
        Prestige layers
              
        💡 Re-platform resets your run for Insights: +3% production each, spent on Architecture and the Tech Radar.
        
              
        📈 Acquisition resets the company (Insights too) for Equity: spent on the Board, which brings automation and new sources.
        
              
        👽 First Contact resets the civilization for Xenobytes: alien tech, alien data types and alien sources.
        
              
        🌀 Big Bang becomes available once everything is data. It starts a new universe with permanent Axioms.
        
              
        A good rule of thumb: prestige when it would roughly double the currency you have.
        
            
        Challenges
              
        Under Prestige → Challenges. Each one is a run with a restriction. Reach the goal and you get a strong reward until the layer above resets; some let you keep upgrades, columns or more through a prestige. You can abandon a challenge at any time.
        
            
        Saving and devices
              
        Progress lives in this browser. To play on several devices, sign in with your email under Save → Cloud save and open the link on each one. The save that has ingested more rows in total wins, and you're asked before anything is overwritten.
        
            
        Install as an app
              
        Rows to Riches can be installed like an app and plays fully offline (cloud sync catches up when you're back online).
        
              
        Desktop Chrome / Edge: Settings → Display → Install, or the install icon in the address bar. Android: browser menu → Install app. iPhone / iPad: Safari's Share button → Add to Home Screen.

        Privacy
        
            
        No ads and no cookies. Cloud save stores your save plus a hash of your account code (or your email, if you chose email sign-in); delete it all any time under Save. Anonymous gameplay stats (session length, which environment you're in, prestige milestones, without any identifier) help balance the game; turn them off under Save → Privacy. Feedback you send is stored with the game context shown in the form, plus your email only if you add one. Nothing else leaves your browser unless you sign in.
        
            
        Everything loads from this site, which runs on Cloudflare: no Google Fonts, CDNs, trackers or embeds. The one extra request is Cloudflare's own cookieless page-view counter. Feedback reaches me as a Telegram message; sign-in mail is sent through Cloudflare.
